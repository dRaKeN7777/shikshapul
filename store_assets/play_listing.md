## Full description

Help your child prepare for entrance examinations with a calm, private and structured study system.

ShikshaPul brings practice questions, mock examinations, mistake review and study planning together in one focused app. It is designed for students preparing for engineering and medical entrance pathways in Nepal, including IOE, KU, PU, PoU and MECEE BL.

Why families can trust ShikshaPul

• No account is required
• No advertisements
• Core learning works offline
• Progress stays on the student’s device
• Clear practice history helps students learn from mistakes

What students can do

• Practise subject and chapter based questions
• Take timed mock examinations
• Review incorrect answers in a mistake notebook
• Identify weaker areas and plan the next study session
• Follow a practical study strategy instead of studying without direction
• Open clearly labelled official learning resources when internet access is available

ShikshaPul is built to support consistent preparation. It does not promise examination results and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or an official service of any university, examination board or government body. Examination details may change, so students should always verify dates, eligibility and notices through the relevant official source.

Prepare privately. Learn from every attempt. Build exam confidence with ShikshaPul.
