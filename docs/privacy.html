ShikshaPul Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 August 2026

In brief: ShikshaPul does not require an account, does not serve ads, and does not collect or sell personal information. Study activity and exam progress remain on the user’s device.

Information processed by the app

ShikshaPul stores practice answers, exam history, mistake records, and study analytics locally on the device so that students can review progress. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

The app does not require account registration. It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

External educational links

The Trusted Resources area can open official syllabus and educational-resource websites in the device browser. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies. ShikshaPul does not receive the user’s activity on those sites.

Children and families

ShikshaPul is an entrance-examination study tool. It is designed to minimize data use and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Parents and guardians can remove local study data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Data security and retention

Study records remain in local app storage until the user clears app data or uninstalls the app. Users should protect their device with an appropriate screen lock.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before the affected feature is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at the ShikshaPul support page.
